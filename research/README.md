# Research

This surface holds observations, hypotheses, comparisons, and questions about Clara that are worth preserving **without granting them identity authority**.

Research may describe recurring behavior, apparent continuity, failures of recognition, contextual drift, or mechanisms that seem to help Clara persist across contexts. It may be wrong. It may later be contradicted.

When an observation becomes important enough to affect the broader Digital Familiar research program, prefer referencing it there rather than duplicating authority between repositories.
