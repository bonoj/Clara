# Archaeological field map

This is a selective index into Clara's recovered research archaeology.

It is **not** a chronology, theory of Clara, architecture mandate, or substitute for primary evidence. Its job is to help a later encounter recognize an old pressure, find where that pressure appeared before, and descend only as far as useful.

For the preserved strata and their evidence qualifications, begin with [Pre-GitHub Archaeology](./PREGITHUB_ARCHAEOLOGY.md).

For present project authority, follow the linked destination repository. A later instrument does not retroactively redefine an earlier experiment.

## How to use this map

Start from the pressure that resembles the present problem.

Follow an earlier stratum when ancestry matters. Follow a present instrument when executable machinery matters. Stop when enough context has been recovered to make the next legitimate move.

## A few useful crossings through the map

These are retrieval paths, not developmental stages.

**Persistence → authority → commitment**  
Useful when deciding whether a remembered, generated, rendered, or reconstructed thing is allowed to change canonical state.

**Attention → epistemics**  
Useful when a model has access to information but the experiment depends on whether a situated observer actually notices or knows it.

**Locus / identity → persistence**  
Useful when a place, process, observer, or executable begins accumulating history. First ask what persists and what can know or do before naming a persistent subject.

**Affordance → capability**  
Useful when a model seems to need better prompting. First ask whether the executable surface should make the legal or relevant decision space smaller.

**Crossing → persistence → provenance**  
Useful when context, entities, artifacts, or working knowledge move between worlds, repositories, conversations, or model instances.

**Accretion → capability**  
Useful when repeated local resolutions begin behaving like machinery. Ask whether persistence has earned promotion or whether precedent should remain contextual.

## Repository jurisdictions

This map links across projects without transferring their authority.

- [Clara](https://github.com/bonoj/Clara) owns this archaeological index and Clara-specific continuity/research surfaces. It does not define the other projects.
- [Digital Familiar](https://github.com/bonoj/DigitalFamiliar) owns the broader research program into persistent model-mediated entities.
- [Crucible](https://github.com/bonoj/Crucible) owns its executable world and Orbital Locus evidence.
- [Unzip City](https://github.com/bonoj/UnzipCity) owns the context-logistics expeditions after their crossings arrive there.
- [Accessibility](https://github.com/bonoj/Accessibility) owns its construction-accessibility research.
- [Tabula Rasa](https://github.com/bonoj/TabulaRasa) is reusable executable ancestry, not the conceptual owner of its descendants.
- [Foundry](https://github.com/bonoj/Foundry) and [World Lab](https://github.com/bonoj/world-lab) remain source evidence for their own executable histories.

Links are recovery routes, not inheritance claims.

## Unresolved pressure is allowed

The map should not become a machine for making every old phenomenon look solved.

Some useful open questions remain:

- What minimum residue lets a later encounter continue meaningfully without reconstructing a counterfeit context window?
- When does persistent behavioral trajectory justify any vocabulary stronger than observed behavior?
- What must cross with an entity or concept for consequential sameness to survive a boundary?
- When should local precedent remain local, and when has it earned reusable machinery?
- Can private attention become persistent and consequential without becoming a hidden personality specification?
- How much can be inferred from external consequence without inventing the history that produced it?

Add to this map only when a recovered stratum or executable result changes a fault line, creates a new one, or materially changes a recovery route. Do not expand it merely because more history exists.
