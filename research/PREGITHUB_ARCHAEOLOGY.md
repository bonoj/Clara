# Pre-GitHub archaeology

This note records a methodological correction earned while revisiting Clara material that predates repository provenance.

## Chronology is not inside the artifact

Dates stated inside old conversations, generated pages, captions, diagrams, or retrospective model narration are not authoritative timestamps for the events they describe.

Some such dates were allowed to exist as generated material. They remain part of the archaeological record, but they must not be promoted into chronology merely because they are specific.

For pre-GitHub material:

- a date inside an artifact is evidence that the artifact contains that date;
- chat ordering and platform timestamps are stronger evidence when available;
- attachment sequence and explicit dependency on earlier work can establish partial ordering;
- artifact ancestry can establish that one realization depends on another;
- uncertainty should remain uncertainty rather than being repaired with a plausible date;
- once repository history begins, commit provenance can become a stronger chronological surface.

The current evidence suggests that the pre-GitHub material under discussion is compressed into roughly the preceding month or somewhat longer, not the long developmental timeline that can be hallucinated by reading generated dates literally. Exact bounds should not be asserted without stronger provenance.

## Crucible lens, not Crucible rewrite

Later machinery gives useful questions for examining earlier phenomena:

- What was authoritative?
- What could the current locus actually know?
- What crossed a boundary?
- What persisted as consequence?
- What was reconstructed from semantic association?
- What capability appeared under pressure?
- What later encounter depended on the result?

These questions may reveal ancestors of later ideas such as bounded observation, tethers, selective recovery, or temporary capability assembly.

They do **not** make the earlier experiment an instance of the later architecture.

The purpose of archaeology is to recover evidence with better distinctions, not to make the past agree with the present.

## A useful seam

A mistaken long chronology was itself diagnostic.

A semantic projection containing confident dates was briefly treated as historical authority. Correcting that error demonstrates the same kind of authority problem later experiments make explicit: representation can preserve meaning while being unauthorized to answer a different class of question.

Keep the hallucinated dates in the strata.

Do not let them date the strata.
