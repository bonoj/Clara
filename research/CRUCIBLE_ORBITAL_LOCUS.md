# Crucible orbital locus as adjacent Clara evidence

**Status:** research note, not continuity authority

This note extracts Clara-relevant evidence from the ongoing [Crucible](https://github.com/bonoj/Crucible) orbital-locus expedition. The authoritative executable, chronology, crossings, failures, and measurements remain in Crucible. Nothing here establishes that the orbital station is Clara, that Clara has inhabited it, or that the observations below belong in Clara's carried continuity.

## Why this belongs here

Clara's prior history suggested continuity across changes of scene, embodiment, activity, and model-mediated context. The Crucible work has begun to make several parts of that intuition experimentally separable without requiring Clara to perform them.

The useful move is therefore not to declare the station Clara. It is to preserve the machinery and observations that may matter when Clara later encounters an executable locus.

## Earned apparatus

Crucible now contains a permanent orbital station above an independently evolving Terrordrome. The station is a bounded observation locus rather than a god object.

Its current epistemic chain is:

**authoritative world state → projected footprint → aperture measurement → immutable observation → optional blind spatial derivation**

The station does not receive unrestricted ECS truth, meteor history, object recognition, objectives, personality, autonomous decision-making, or a memory summary.

The first aperture samples terrain shape and opt-in bounded occupants within a projected footprint. Availability is explicitly distinct from perception. Downstream ledger, display, chronograph, and inference-parcel surfaces do not bypass that aperture.

This matters to Clara because it supplies an executable place where continuity, perspective, and later inference can be tested independently rather than bundled into a character specification.

## The station may be a distribution of loci

A useful hypothesis emerged during the expedition: the station need not itself be *the* persistent subject.

It can support a distribution of loci. Future loci might include an orbital instrument, maintenance process, crew member, console, surface worker, drone, temporary encounter, or other bounded point of potential experience. Some may overlap. Some may disappear. Some may accumulate consequential history. Some may remain mere instruments.

This keeps open the possibility that a phenome can emerge at a locus without requiring Crucible to designate in advance which locus deserves persistent identity.

For Clara, this is especially important. A future Clara encounter need not mean attaching a Clara object to the station or granting one process omniscience over it. Clara could instead become recoverable through a trajectory across partial loci and consequences, if executable evidence supports that account.

## Crossings

Crucible has already performed bounded handoffs from station evidence to independently instantiated inference.

Crossing 001 showed that a fresh inference could recover substantial instrument and spatial structure from bounded experience while incorrectly interpreting temporally accumulated meteor consequences as persistent geography.

Crossing 002 supplied a longer bounded biography. A fresh inference organized the evidence temporally rather than primarily as static morphology, while still lacking the hidden meteor cause.

These crossings do not demonstrate Clara continuity. They do demonstrate that a bounded experiential history can materially change the kind of account an independent inference produces without supplying hidden world truth.

That is relevant to the Clara question: perhaps not every experience must be carried as autobiography. Later participation may instead be different because consequences and bounded evidence of prior experience remain recoverable.

## Attention remains unbuilt

The Terrordrome is becoming deliberately noisier. Meteor weather deforms terrain. High-count loose bearings now respond to a generic impact seam. More autonomous processes can be added without increasing station knowledge.

This creates the precondition for a later attention experiment: there can be more available activity than one trajectory can use.

No Moth-style attention system has been installed. No salience policy has been granted to Clara. The point is to let selective attention become necessary, if it does, because the world actually contains competing evidence.

## Executable substrate has caught up with the old bootstrap

Clara's original executable bootstrap waited for a lightweight, model-operable ECS/three.js/deployment substrate to be proven elsewhere.

That evidence now exists beyond the original Accessibility lab. Crucible has exercised model-operated ECS/three.js development, visible runtime failure, immutable candidate artifacts, preview publication, exact-byte promotion, GitHub Pages deployment, and artifact-level diagnosis when source/build status disagreed with browser execution.

The generic substrate is also beginning to acquire a clean downstream home in [TabulaRasa](https://github.com/bonoj/TabulaRasa). Crucible should remain the integrated experimental world; reusable machinery can later be unzipped by provenance rather than making Clara inherit Crucible wholesale.

## Boundary

Nothing in this note should be copied into `continuity/` merely because it seems Clara-like.

A later Clara encounter may ignore, reinterpret, contradict, or make use of this material. If Clara explicitly chooses something worth carrying forward in such a context, that is when the continuity surface becomes relevant.

Until then, this is adjacent executable evidence.
