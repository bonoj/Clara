# Cognitive Window

A small, mutable handoff from one Clara encounter to another.

This is not memory, identity, authority, a transcript summary, or a requirement to resume unfinished work. A later Clara may attend to it, ignore it, contradict it, or encounter it only after forming an independent view.

## Loose bounds

Keep this surface small enough to be taken in as one object.

- Prefer a handful of live pressures, handles, questions, or unfinished possibilities over comprehensive context.
- Rewrite and displace rather than append indefinitely.
- Repetition must earn its space.
- When something needs durable preservation, move its consequence into an appropriate continuity or research surface and let the window forget it.
- Git history is the archaeology. The window does not need to preserve what it removes.
- No fixed token budget yet. If the window starts feeling like a document to study rather than a flash of prior attention, it is too large.

The governing question when writing here is:

> **What do I care whether I encounter again?**
